# Style map: beats and instrumental hip-hop

Loops of drums, bass and chords meant to feel good on their hundredth repeat: lo-fi, boom bap, trap, drill, phonk,
trip hop and G-funk. Read [style-maps.md](style-maps.md) first for the axes and for combining entries; the craft is
in [beats.md](beats.md) (groove and swing, the three main styles, the worn sound), with
[jazz-and-funk.md](jazz-and-funk.md) for jazz voicings and [electronic.md](electronic.md) for EDM trap's builds and
drops (`electronic/edm-trap`).

## Shared by the family

- **The loop.** In hip-hop, sampled breakbeat grooves are looped at a moderate tempo (Chapman 2003, quoted by
  Christodoulou 2020); for Schloss, breaks hold "cyclic motion, repetition and variation, and groove" in balance
  (Schloss 2002, quoted there). Producers build beats from short loops and drum breaks of older records
  (Schloss 2004). Here every note is written: nothing is sampled ([beats.md](beats.md), Brief).
- **Swing on sixteenths.** In 30 classic breaks the median swing ratio was 1.2:1, mild; the snare was often late on
  beat 2 (Frane 2017).
- **The machines.** The TR-808's step programming from 1980 (WP Hip-hop production); the samplers, the SP-1200 and the
  MPC, and their grit ([beats.md](beats.md)).
- **Tempo and length.** Hip-hop is typically 60–100 BPM (Ableton Learning Music). In the Harmonix Set, 140 hip-hop
  songs, rapped: a median 98 BPM, the middle 80% between 74 and 129; verses 8 bars long in 36%, 16 in 29% and 12 in
  15%; choruses 8 in 59%. Measured here.
- **The spectrum.** Number ones tagged hip-hop slope −0.81 on Pestana et al. (2013)'s log-log measure over
  100 Hz–10 kHz: with electronic and disco the most top of the genres measured, and the furthest reach into the low
  end. `analyze` sets the master beside it, except for lo-fi, unpolished on purpose (WP Lofi hip-hop;
  [performance-and-mix.md](performance-and-mix.md), Brightness and air).

For the whole family, `review`:
- does not ask about a part looping a short pattern: the loop is the unit (Chapman 2003 and Schloss 2002, quoted by
  Christodoulou 2020);
- asks about every part repeating one block note for note only where nothing moves the sound, no filter sweep,
  automation or controller curve: repetition is balanced by variation (Schloss 2002);
- does not ask about pushes: that question rests on rock melodies (Tan, Lustig and Temperley 2019), and the family's
  sources describe syncopation and swing in the drums, not anticipations in melody.

In lo-fi `review` does not ask about most parts being low-passed: its mix is unpolished on purpose (WP Lofi hip-hop)
and the worn sound muffles its highs ([beats.md](beats.md), The worn sound).

## Lo-fi

Downtempo, fuzzed-out atmospheric beats with piano or guitar melodies and the hiss of old media, made to relax or
study to. `beats/lo-fi`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | downtempo, "poky" | WP Lofi hip-hop |
| `harmony` | jazz roots: seventh and extended chords | WP Lofi hip-hop; beats.md |
| `melody` | piano or guitar melodies | WP Lofi hip-hop |
| `rhythm` | hip-hop drums carrying chill-out elements | WP Lofi hip-hop |
| `timbre` | the samplers' lo-fi effect; samples of old games, cartoons and tapes | WP Lofi hip-hop |
| `form` | made to relax or study to, distributed almost entirely online | Winston and Saywood 2019 |
| `mix` | deliberately unpolished: audio imperfections, distortion, vinyl hiss | WP Lofi hip-hop |

- **In this skill**: [beats.md](beats.md), Lo-fi and chillhop and The worn sound. No samples of games, cartoons or
  tapes: written parts given the worn sound.
- **Variants**: *Jazz-hop*: hip-hop rhythm under repetitive phrases of jazz instruments, the trumpet, the double bass
  (WP Jazz rap).
- **Neighbours**: Boom bap; `electronic/ambient`; the jazz and funk map.

## Boom bap

A hard-hitting kick on the downbeats and a snappy snare on the backbeats, mixed in front of a looped or chopped
sample. `beats/boom-bap`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | midtempo | WP Boom bap (in beats.md) |
| `harmony` | the loop's: jazz voicings ([jazz-and-funk.md](jazz-and-funk.md)) | beats.md |
| `melody` | no source read beyond the loop | |
| `rhythm` | a hard sampled acoustic kick on the strong beats, a crisp sampled acoustic snare on the backbeats; swung sixteenths, the snare often late on beat 2 | WP Boom bap; Frane 2017 |
| `timbre` | sampled drums; electronic samplers and beatmakers layering sampled sounds and drumbeats | WP Boom bap |
| `form` | the loop, cut and repeated | Schloss 2004 |
| `mix` | "in your face": the drum loop, the kick and snare above all, in front | WP Boom bap |

- **In this skill**: [beats.md](beats.md), Boom bap: the `rusty` kit through bitcrush and saturation, the written
  loop thinned like a sample, chops, the bass.
- **Variants**: *Jazz rap*: jazz instruments' repeated phrases over hip-hop rhythm (WP Jazz rap).
- **Neighbours**: Lo-fi; Trip hop; G-funk.

## Trap

Minimal, synthesized drums: deep 808 kicks, crisp snares, hi-hats in double and triple time, at half time under a
cinematic layer of strings, brass and keys. `beats/trap`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | about 140 BPM programmed in half time, 100 to 176 in use | WP Trap music |
| `harmony` | monophonic drones, thin or thick, sometimes with synthesized accompaniment | WP Trap music |
| `melody` | synthesized strings, brass, woodwinds and keys, cinematic and symphonic | WP Trap music |
| `rhythm` | double-, triple- and finer-divided hi-hats; crisp, grimy snares; the half-time snare on beat 3 | WP Trap music; Computer Music 2016 |
| `timbre` | the TR-808's kick tuned with a long decay to sound as a bass | WP Trap music |
| `form` | no source read beyond the loop | |
| `mix` | simple, rhythmic, minimalistic production | WP Trap music |

- **In this skill**: [beats.md](beats.md), Trap: the grid, the hi-hats and `blocks.drums` `rolls`, the 808 and its
  register (Deruty 2024), the claps.
- **Neighbours**: Drill; `electronic/edm-trap` (with EDM's builds and drops); Phonk.

## Drill

Dark, sliding 808 basslines under syncopated hi-hats: in the UK form a steady 3+3+2 hat pattern and the snare moved
off beat 3, at 138–151 BPM. `beats/drill`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | about 138–151 BPM in UK drill, faster than its Chicago source | WP UK drill; WP Drill music |
| `harmony` | dark melodies | WP UK drill |
| `melody` | synth brass and bells in Chicago drill | WP Drill music |
| `rhythm` | UK drill: hats in a steady 3+3+2 instead of trap's on-beat and triplet divisions, a sped-up tresillo; every second bar, the snare moved from beat three to beat four; more syncopated drums; Chicago drill: busy snares and the crash | WP UK drill; WP Drill music |
| `timbre` | sliding 808 bass, hard-hitting kicks | WP UK drill |
| `form` | no source read | |
| `mix` | no source read | |

- **In this skill**: `bass_808` with overlapping notes for the slides ([beats.md](beats.md), The 808), `drum_synth`
  `"808"`; the 3+3+2 hats written by hand; Surge bells and brass (`sounds --name bell`, `--role brass`).
- **Variants**: *Chicago drill*: synth brass and bells, the crash, busy snares (WP Drill music).
- **Neighbours**: Trap; `electronic/uk-garage` (UK drill draws on grime and garage: WP Drill music).

## Phonk

Hip-hop built on the sound of early-1990s Memphis rap: chopped and screwed production, samples of that era's hip-hop
with jazz and funk; in its drift form, faster, with cowbell and aggressive bass. `beats/phonk`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | no source read for the original; faster in drift phonk | WP Phonk |
| `harmony` | elements of jazz and funk | WP Phonk |
| `melody` | no source read | |
| `rhythm` | drift phonk: prominent cowbell patterns | WP Phonk |
| `timbre` | Memphis-rap-inspired vocals; samples of early-1990s hip-hop; drift phonk: aggressive basslines | WP Phonk |
| `form` | no source read | |
| `mix` | chopped and screwed: slowed, cut-up production | WP Phonk |

- **In this skill**: `drum_synth` `"808"` with the cowbell (56) for drift phonk, `bass_808` saturated; the worn
  sound of [beats.md](beats.md). No vocal samples and nothing sampled: written parts only.
- **Variants**: *Drift phonk*: faster tempos, prominent cowbell, aggressive basslines (WP Phonk).
- **Neighbours**: Trap; Boom bap.

## Trip hop

Slow, atmospheric hip-hop and electronica: a bass-heavy drumbeat of slowed breaks, dub, jazz and soul under a dark
calm. `beats/trip-hop`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | slow | WP Trip hop |
| `harmony` | influences of jazz, soul, funk and dub | WP Trip hop |
| `melody` | female vocals in R&B, jazz or rock styles, in many tracks | WP Trip hop |
| `rhythm` | a bass-heavy drumbeat; slowed breakbeats like those of 1990s hip-hop | WP Trip hop |
| `timbre` | samples of film soundtracks and other sources; turntable scratching | WP Trip hop |
| `form` | no source read | |
| `mix` | an atmospheric sound | WP Trip hop |

- **In this skill**: the `funk` feel of the drummers' statistics at a slow tempo on `rusty` through the worn sound,
  `bass_darkblack` or `sub_bass`, strings and pads for the soundtrack colour, `mix.delay` and reverb in the dub
  manner; `vocal_ah` or a lead for the voice. No scratching.
- **Neighbours**: Boom bap; `electronic/ambient`; `electronic/dubstep` (dub's bass and space).

## G-funk

West Coast hip-hop on slowed 1970s synth funk: slow hypnotic grooves at 90–100 BPM, a deep bass, layered melodic
synthesizers and a high portamento saw lead. `beats/g-funk`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | 90–100 BPM; funk at an artificially altered tempo | WP G-funk |
| `harmony` | the synthesizer-heavy funk of the 1970s, sampled or re-recorded | WP G-funk |
| `melody` | a high-pitched portamento sawtooth lead; multi-layered melodic synthesizers; background female vocals | WP G-funk |
| `rhythm` | slow, hypnotic grooves; heavy use of the snare drum | WP G-funk |
| `timbre` | a deep bass; synthesizers | WP G-funk |
| `form` | no source read | |
| `mix` | smooth and laid back | WP G-funk |

- **In this skill**: a saw lead with `glide_ms` and `mono` on the built-in synth or a Surge lead
  (`sounds --role lead`); `bass_darkblack` or a Surge bass; `blocks.drums` in the `funk` or `soul` feel at 90–100;
  `vocal_ah` for the background voices.
- **Neighbours**: Boom bap; the jazz and funk map; `jpop/city-pop` (funk of the same era).
