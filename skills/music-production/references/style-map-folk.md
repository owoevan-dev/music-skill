# Style map: folk and roots traditions

Dance-tune and string-band traditions of Europe and North America: Celtic music, klezmer, bluegrass and country. Read
[style-maps.md](style-maps.md) first for the axes and for combining entries; the instruments are in
[soundbanks.md](soundbanks.md), where what the banks lack is plain: no bagpipes, accordion, concertina, banjo,
mandolin, bouzouki, cimbalom or steel guitar.

[Bluegrass](#bluegrass) · [Country](#country) · [Sources](#sources)

## Shared by the family

- **The fiddle.** Every entry here has it: the `vsco` solo violin (0:40) and the `iowa_strings` violin play it, with
  `performance.legato` and short notes for the bowing.
- **Stand-ins**, this skill's choice: the `vcsl_world` strumstick, a fretted dulcimer, for plucked folk strings; the
  `vcsl_winds` recorders and ocarina for a whistle; the `vcsl` and `vcsl_world` harps; the `vcsl_world` frame drums for
  the bodhrán. Name each in `decisions`.
- **The spectrum.** Number ones tagged folk slope −1.18 on Pestana et al. (2013)'s log-log measure over 100 Hz–10 kHz
  (jazz −1.29, pop −0.94): acoustic music falls away at the top. `analyze` sets the master beside it
  ([performance-and-mix.md](performance-and-mix.md), Brightness and air).

For the family, `review` keeps every default: no source read describes a limit to change.

## Celtic

The folk music of the Celtic nations: dance tunes on fiddle, pipes, whistle, flute and harp, the strathspey in
Scotland, harp melodies with a sung counter-melody in Wales. `folk/celtic`

- **In this skill**: the fiddle, a flute or recorder, the harps, a frame drum; the piano for the Maritimes' fiddle
  accompaniment. No pipes.
- **Neighbours**: Bluegrass (the fiddle tune across the Atlantic); `orchestral/baroque` (its dance forms).

## Klezmer

The Jewish wedding and dance music of Eastern Europe: dance tunes, ritual melodies and virtuosic pieces, played with
ornaments that imitate sighing and laughing. `folk/klezmer`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | no source read | |
| `harmony` | accompaniment from octaves without harmony to a second violin's partial chords to harmonised brass band arrangements | WP Klezmer |
| `melody` | ornaments shared with cantorial and Hasidic singing, sighs and laughs; ornament chosen with taste and restraint | WP Klezmer |
| `rhythm` | dance tunes; the bulgar, a circle dance with a recognisable syncopated rhythm in 2/4 or 4/4 | WP Klezmer |
| `timbre` | a lead violin, second violin, cello and cimbalom; the clarinet from the mid-19th century; in larger bands cornets, trombones, contrabass and a large drum | WP Klezmer |
| `form` | music for weddings and social functions; elements of Ottoman, Baroque, German and Slavic dance and Jewish religious music | WP Klezmer |
| `mix` | no source read | |

- **In this skill**: the violin, the `vsco` clarinet (0:71) with `bend` for the sighs, `cello_bigcat`, `vsco` brass and
  the bass drum; no cimbalom, the pianos or a harp stand in.
- **Neighbours**: Celtic (dance tunes on the fiddle without klezmer's vocal ornaments).

## Bluegrass

Acoustic string-band music: fiddle, mandolin, five-string banjo, guitar and upright bass taking turns on the melody,
fast breakdowns. `folk/bluegrass`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | rapid tempos in breakdowns | WP Bluegrass music |
| `harmony` | sometimes complex chord changes in breakdowns | WP Bluegrass music |
| `melody` | one or more players take a turn on the melody and improvise around it while the others accompany | WP Bluegrass music |
| `rhythm` | the off-beat "driven", close to the previous bass note, or "swung", farther from it | WP Bluegrass music |
| `timbre` | acoustic instruments only: fiddle, five-string banjo, guitar, mandolin, upright bass, often the resonator guitar, occasionally harmonica | WP Bluegrass music |
| `form` | breakdowns: rapid tempos and instrumental dexterity | WP Bluegrass music |
| `mix` | no source read | |

- **In this skill**: the fiddle, `iowa_guitar` or `guitar_shiny` (microphone 0:25), `bass_sneaky`, the strumstick for
  the banjo's and mandolin's place, the harmonica of `vcsl_winds`; `performance.swing` with a small ratio for the
  swung off-beat. No banjo or mandolin.
- **Neighbours**: Country; Celtic.

## Country

Ballads and dance tunes of the American South: fiddles, banjos, mandolins, harmonicas and guitars, acoustic,
electric, steel and resonator. `folk/country`

| `harmony` | traditional harmonies | WP Country music |
| `melody` | ballads and dance tunes | WP Country music |
| `rhythm` | no source read | |
| `timbre` | banjos, mandolins, fiddles, harmonicas and guitars, acoustic, electric, steel or resonator; the steel guitar from Hawaiian music | WP Country music |
| `form` | on the same songs: verses 8 bars long in 59%, choruses 8 in 39%; a bridge in 72%; a median 3.7 minutes | Nieto et al. 2019 |
| `mix` | no source read | |

- **In this skill**: the fiddle, `fsbs_clean` and the acoustic guitars, the harmonica, `bass_darkblack`, `rusty`; a
  steel guitar's slides as `bend` on a clean guitar (this skill's approximation).
- **Neighbours**: Bluegrass; `rock/classic-hard`; `pop/ballad`.
