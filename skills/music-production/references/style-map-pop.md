# Style map: pop and ballads

Song-based pop as an instrumental: up-tempo pop, the ballad, disco, synth-pop and hyperpop, each with its vocal line
on an instrument. Read [style-maps.md](style-maps.md) first for the axes and for combining entries; the craft is in
[pop-and-ballad.md](pop-and-ballad.md) (form, harmony, the lead that sings, the mix), with
[electronic.md](electronic.md) and [electronic-sounds.md](electronic-sounds.md) for the synth styles. J-pop is its own
map ([style-map-jpop.md](style-map-jpop.md)).

## Shared by the family

- **The song.** Pop is made of repeated choruses and hooks in short to medium-length songs, often in verse–chorus
  form, the chorus contrasting with the verse and built to stick by repetition (WP Pop music). The pre-chorus builds
  momentum (Summach 2011); the chorus is more intense than the verse, denser or higher (Summach 2012, in OMT).
- **Chords.** I, IV, V and vi in the four-chord schemas, doo-wop, singer/songwriter and hopscotch (OMT, Four-Chord
  Schemas); IV and ♭VII before the tonic (de Clercq and Temperley 2011). The key change near the end, the most common
  of the 1958–1990 number ones, has nearly gone from them since 2010 (Dalla Riva 2022, 2025).
- **The intro.** Instrumental intros of US top-10 singles shrank from about 20 seconds in the mid-1980s to about 5
  (Léveillé Gauvin 2018).
- **Measured.** In the Harmonix Set, 422 songs labelled pop: a median 118 BPM, the middle 80% between 78 and 135;
  a median 3.5 minutes (80% between 2.7 and 4.3); verses 8 bars long in 51% and 16 in 22%, choruses 8 in 49% and 16
  in 18%, prechoruses 8 in 53% and 4 in 30%; three choruses at the median, and a bridge in 75% of the songs.
  Measured here; the set leans toward danceable pop.
- **The spectrum.** US and UK number-one singles tagged pop slope −0.94 on Pestana et al. (2013)'s log-log measure over
  100 Hz–10 kHz, disco −0.80 (the `disco` entry's reference) and rock −0.98: the steeper, the less top. `analyze`
  sets the master beside it ([performance-and-mix.md](performance-and-mix.md), Brightness and air).

`review` keeps every default for the family: its questions on restatement and pushes rest on pop and rock melodies
(Summach 2011; Tan, Lustig and Temperley 2019).

## Up-tempo pop

The verse–chorus pop song at a dance tempo: a simple beat and melody, a chorus that sticks. `pop/uptempo`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | rhythms or tempos that can easily be danced to | WP Pop music |
| `harmony` | simple, with limited harmonic accompaniment; the four-chord schemas | WP Pop music; OMT |
| `melody` | catchy hooks; a chorus that contrasts with the verse in melody, rhythm and harmony | WP Pop music |
| `rhythm` | a consistent, noticeable rhythmic element; dance-oriented rhythms | WP Pop music (Warner) |
| `timbre` | an emphasis on recording, production and technology over live performance | WP Pop music (Warner) |
| `form` | songs of two and a half to three and a half minutes; verse–chorus or thirty-two-bar form; the chorus often after a drop, where bass and drums drop out | WP Pop music |
| `mix` | no source read beyond this skill's delivery at −14 LUFS ([pop-and-ballad.md](pop-and-ballad.md), The mix) | |

- **In this skill**: [pop-and-ballad.md](pop-and-ballad.md); the drums from `blocks.drums` in the `rock` or `soul`
  feel or the `dance` feel for dance-pop ([building-blocks.md](building-blocks.md)).
- **Variants**: *Dance-pop*: up-tempo, for the club and the radio at once, strong beats and easy, uncomplicated
  structures nearer pop than dance music, from disco, post-disco and synth-pop (WP Dance-pop).
- **Neighbours**: Ballad; Disco; `jpop/anime-opening`.

## Ballad

The slow, sentimental song: a lush arrangement that puts the melody and its harmony in front. `pop/ballad`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | usually slow | WP Sentimental ballad |
| `harmony` | the harmonies emphasised by a lush arrangement | WP Sentimental ballad |
| `melody` | the melody emphasised; a lead that phrases like a voice ([pop-and-ballad.md](pop-and-ballad.md), Melody for the lead) | WP Sentimental ballad |
| `rhythm` | no source read | |
| `timbre` | acoustic instruments, guitars, pianos, saxophones, sometimes an orchestra | WP Sentimental ballad |
| `form` | verse–chorus with a pre-chorus; the final chorus bigger, layers held back for it | OMT; Senior 2024 |
| `mix` | no source read beyond this skill's delivery at −14 LUFS | |

- **In this skill**: [pop-and-ballad.md](pop-and-ballad.md): the example layout, the lead, the strings, the pianos.
- **Variants**:
  - *Power ballad*: love or heartache shifting into wordless intensity with heavy drums and a distorted guitar solo
    (WP Sentimental ballad).
  - *Soft rock*: simple, melodic songs with big, lush productions, mostly ballads, from singer-songwriter and pop
    rock (WP Soft rock; WP Sentimental ballad).
- **Neighbours**: Up-tempo pop; `jpop/enka`; the Romantic orchestral entry for its strings (`orchestral/romantic`).

## Disco

Four-on-the-floor dance music with syncopated octave basslines, strings and horns over a pad of electric pianos and
scratching guitars. `pop/disco`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | measured here on 14 Harmonix Set songs labelled funk/disco: a median 119 BPM, the middle 80% between 109 and 131 | Nieto et al. 2019 |
| `harmony` | major and minor seventh chords, more often found in jazz than in pop; electric pianos and muted rhythm guitars as the pad that defines the progression | WP Disco |
| `melody` | soaring, reverberant vocals doubled by horns; strings and horns playing linear phrases with them or fills between; less lead guitar than rock | WP Disco |
| `rhythm` | a steady four-on-the-floor bass drum, eighth or sixteenth hi-hats with an open hat on the off-beat; a heavy, syncopated bassline in broken octaves; Latin percussion | WP Disco |
| `timbre` | a large band: several chordal instruments, drum kit and percussion, a horn section, a string orchestra, solo flute or piccolo; the Rhodes, Wurlitzer and Clavinet; "chicken-scratch" guitar, the strings lightly muted and strummed near the bridge | WP Disco |
| `form` | on the same songs: choruses 8 bars long in 69%, verses 8 in 60%; four choruses at the median | Nieto et al. 2019 |
| `mix` | multitrack production; loud hi-hats | WP Disco |

- **In this skill**: `blocks.drums` in the `dance` feel with `kick="four_floor"` and `pulse="offbeat"`;
  `bass_darkblack` in broken octaves; `vsco_strings` and `vsco` brass; electric pianos (`studio.py sounds --name ep`);
  muted guitar chords on `fsbs_clean`, short (`gate` about 0.3) and strummed (this skill's approximation of the
  scratch); the flute and piccolo of `vsco`; congas and bongos in `vcsl_world`.
- **Neighbours**: `electronic/house` (disco's descendant); `jpop/city-pop`; Up-tempo pop.

## Synth-pop

Pop made with synthesizers, drum machines and sequencers, synthetic textures and a robotic rigidity in place of rock's
playing. `pop/synth-pop`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | no source read | |
| `harmony` | no source read | |
| `melody` | lines shaped by monophonic synthesizers in the early style | WP Synth-pop |
| `rhythm` | drum machines and sequencers; "robotic rigidity" in place of rock's rhythms; later, dance beats | WP Synth-pop |
| `timbre` | the synthesizer dominant, sometimes replacing every other instrument; synthesizers imitating orchestras and horns | WP Synth-pop |
| `form` | rock's structures eschewed | WP Synth-pop |
| `mix` | no source read | |

- **In this skill**: the `synth` and `surge` engines with `drum_synth` ([electronic-sounds.md](electronic-sounds.md));
  `blocks.drums` with `programmed=True`; Surge string and brass patches (`sounds --role brass`, `--name string`).
- **Variants**: *Electropop*: a harder, more electronic sound (WP Synth-pop).
- **Neighbours**: `electronic/synthwave`; `electronic/electro`; `jpop/fast-electro`.

## Hyperpop

An exaggerated, maximalist pop: brash synth melodies, heavy compression and distortion, metallic percussion, catchy
choruses, short songs. `pop/hyperpop`

| Axis | Traits | From |
|---|---|---|
| `tempo` | no source read | |
| `harmony` | no source read | |
| `melody` | brash synth melodies; catchy choruses; pitch-shifted synths | WP Hyperpop |
| `rhythm` | elements of electronic, hip-hop and dance music | WP Hyperpop |
| `timbre` | metallic, melodic percussion sounds; heavily processed voices | WP Hyperpop |
| `form` | short songs | WP Hyperpop |
| `mix` | excessive compression and distortion | WP Hyperpop |

- **In this skill**: bright Surge leads and plucks, `fm_bell` for the metallic percussion, `mix.saturate` and the
  `loud` master; no processed vocals, so a lead takes the hook.
- **Neighbours**: `electronic/future-bass` (its kawaii variant); `jpop/fast-electro`.
